COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions before we get started?

Here are answers to some common questions about our web development services.

Ask Us Directly ↗
How much does a website cost?

The cost depends on the number of pages, design requirements, features and overall project scope. Contact us with your idea for a tailored quotation.

How long does it take to build a website?

The timeline depends on the complexity of the project, content availability and required features. We can discuss an estimated timeline after understanding your requirements.

Can you make my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Responsive design is an important consideration so visitors can use the website on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Can you improve a website I already have?

Yes. We can discuss design updates, content changes, usability improvements and additional functionality for an existing website.

Do I need a domain name and hosting?

A public website generally needs a way to be accessed online. Depending on your project, this may involve a domain name and hosting. We can discuss suitable arrangements with you.

How do I request a website?

Contact us through WhatsApp and tell us about your business, the type of website you need and any features you have in mind. We can then discuss the next steps.
